## ⭐ 作图方法论（2026-08-14 起强制遵循）—《星际穿越》式大画幅宇宙感

> 来源：小红书笔记「如何做出星际穿越风格的图片？」（作者观点：不断加星云/行星/光效 → 只会像科幻壁纸；**先删掉装饰，再建立尺度**）。
> 金句：*真正决定画面是否宏大的，不是写了多少个 "epic"，而是观众能不能在一秒内看懂：人有多小，宇宙有多大。*

**所有生图（尤其飞船/戴森云/恒星尺度的画面）必须包含以下 4 条，缺一不可：**

1. **极小的尺度参照物** — 画面里必须有宇航员/飞船/无人机/居住站等参照物，且**小到接近消失**。宇宙的宏大靠人与它的比例体现，不是天体自己说出来的。
2. **突破画面边界的巨型天体** — 不完整展示行星/飞船/戴森云，让主体**超出画面边缘**，观众看不见全貌，尺度才显得无法估计。
3. **一个明确的真实光源** — 只保留恒星、地平线或引擎光作为主光源；**减少彩色星云与装饰性霓虹**（与旧版“绚烂紫蓝色星云背景”相反——旧版违反此条）。
4. **具有重量的物理材质** — 金属要有磨损与划痕、冰面要有裂纹、岩石要粗糙、飞船要有辐射与微陨石撞击痕；画面要像真实存在的物质，不是漂浮的魔法特效。

**提示词骨架（每次套用）**：`微小参照物＋巨型天体＋明确机位＋单一光源＋真实材质＋大画幅电影镜头`

**落地规则（重做 006-008 时执行）**：删掉旧提示词里的“绚烂星云”“彩色光效”“史诗级”等虚词，改为：参照物明确到米（如“直径 3 米的维修艇”“舱外行走的宇航员”）、主体切出画面边缘、主光源单一（恒星或引擎）、材质细节具象（磨损/结霜/撞击坑）。

### 补充技巧（2026-08-14 第 2 篇）— 镜头语言锚定：用真实摄影设备/摄影师锚定质感

> 来源：小红书「153 | 赛博未来」（Midjourney 参考）。用**电影摄影机型号 + 摄影指导风格**代替“epic/电影感”这类虚词，画面质感会直接对齐真实电影：
> `shot in the style of Arri Alexa Mini and directed in the style of Roger Deakins`（阿莱 Alexa Mini 摄影机 + 罗杰·狄金斯摄影风格）

- **常用摄影机锚**：`Arri Alexa Mini`（数字电影机）、`IMAX 70mm`（大画幅）、`Hasselblad`（中画幅）
- **常用摄影指导锚**：`Roger Deakins`（狄金斯：《银翼杀手2049》《1917》，自然光+大反差）、`Emmanuel Lubezki`（卢贝斯基：《地心引力》《荒野猎人》，长镜头）、`Hoyte van Hoytema`（霍伊特玛：《星际穿越》《奥本海默》，IMAX 实拍）
- **机位锚**：`high-angle shot`（俯拍，显渺小）/ `low-angle shot`（仰拍，显压迫）/ `long lens compression`（长焦压缩，显巨大）
- **套用规则**：世代飞船/戴森云画面 → `IMAX 70mm, shot in the style of Hoyte van Hoytema`（《星际穿越》同款）；城市画面 → `Arri Alexa Mini, directed in the style of Roger Deakins`

### 补充技巧（2026-08-14 第 3 篇）— 干净极繁主义：主体锁死 + 光影减法 + 精准细节词

> 来源：小红书「AI画不出“干净又极繁”的科幻场景？」。细节少了没味儿，细节多了乱糟糟——解法是**干净极繁（clean maximalism）**：

1. **主体锁死，别贪多** — 关键词明确“**XX 为绝对视觉主体**”，巨型空间站/歼星舰当锚点，其他元素只做背景点缀；**避免同时出现多个大型物体**。
2. **光影做减法，对比拉满** — 只用 **1-2 个强光源**（引擎光/恒星光线），主体**一半被照亮、一半沉在阴影**；背景用**纯黑深空/云海**直接过滤多余元素。
3. **细节词要精准，别瞎堆** — 不写“很多细节”，写 **“装甲模块、管线、能量纹路、符文刻痕”** 这类具体工业细节；用 **“虚幻引擎5渲染、次世代材质、电影级光影”** 让细节有质感不杂乱。

**可直接套用的关键词模板**：`硬科幻太空史诗，原画风格，虚幻引擎5渲染，【主体】为绝对视觉主体，极繁工业细节（装甲模块、管线、符文纹路），超广角镜头，超强烈明暗对比，冷白强光与纯黑深空/云海形成极致反差，画面干净无冗余，宏大尺度感，电影级光影，次世代质感，8K超清`

## ① 仿生共生城市 —「城市不是造的,是种的」

### 中文版
未来的城市不是被建造的,而是被培育的。巨大的仿生建筑像参天巨树一样从大地生长,高度超过三百米,树干状的塔楼表面覆盖着会发出淡绿色荧光的苔藓与攀爬藤蔓;建筑的枝桠间悬挂着玻璃穹顶,穹顶内部是茂密的垂直森林,可见多层水培农田与步道。一群白色无人机像蜂群一样在楼宇之间轻柔穿行,它们细小柔和的光点在黄昏中缓慢移动,正在为植物授粉与浇水。城市边缘没有清晰的边界——混凝土小路逐渐被草甸吞没,远处是雾气缭绕的原始森林。黄昏时分,最后一缕阳光斜射,建筑表面的生物荧光与夕阳的橘色交织,温暖的光点如萤火虫般缓缓流动。空气中有薄雾,远处山峦剪影。

摄影级写实,电影级光影,广角镜头,宏伟而宁静的尺度感,人与自然的共生美学,细节丰富,8K。

### English Prompt
A future city that is grown rather than built. Giant biomimetic towers rise from the earth like ancient trees, over 300 meters tall, their trunk-like surfaces covered in softly glowing green bioluminescent moss and climbing vines. Between the branches of these towers hang glass domes containing lush vertical forests with multi-level hydroponic farms and walkways. A swarm of small white drones drifts gently between the buildings like a flock of bees, their tiny soft light points moving slowly in the dusk, pollinating plants and watering crops. The city has no clear edge — paved paths gradually dissolve into meadows, and misty primeval forest fades into the distance. At golden hour, the last sunlight rakes across the scene, interweaving warm orange sunset light with the buildings' bioluminescent green glow, light particles flowing slowly like fireflies. Thin fog in the air, mountain silhouettes on the horizon.

Photorealistic, cinematic lighting, wide-angle, grand yet serene sense of scale, symbiotic human-nature aesthetic, rich detail, 8K.

### Negative
cyberpunk, dystopia, dark, dirty, smog, crowded slums, neon signs, robots with weapons, destroyed buildings

## ② 世代飞船·200年环 —「把一颗小行星雕成家园」

### 中文版
深空之中,一座巨大的环形世代飞船正在缓慢旋转,圆环外径约五十公里,像一颗被精心雕琢的小行星。环壁表面是一圈圈同心条纹:农业区的绿色麦田网格、居住区的暖黄色城市灯火、以及深蓝色的水体反射带,像大地的指纹。透过飞船半透明的环壁,隐约可见内部的完整世界——旋转的居住区、森林、湖泊,还有沿环壁内侧蜿蜒的透明走廊,人们在其中行走。飞船中心轴线上悬浮着一个透明的重力平衡舱,像一只眼睛注视着自己的世界。飞船尾部,聚变引擎喷射出纤细的淡蓝色等离子尾焰,在真空中无声延展数百公里。背景是绚烂的紫蓝色星云、明亮的恒星和一条倾斜的银河。冷色调的宇宙与飞船内部的暖色生命形成鲜明对比,极远处有一颗小小的蓝色行星——那是他们再也回不去的故乡。

史诗级构图,硬科幻工程美学,电影感,高动态范围,细节精致。

### English Prompt
In deep space, a colossal ring-shaped generation ship rotates slowly, its outer diameter about fifty kilometers, resembling a meticulously carved asteroid. The ring's surface shows concentric bands: green farmland grids, warm yellow city lights of residential zones, and dark blue reflective bands of water — like the fingerprint of a world. Through the ship's translucent ring walls, a complete inner world is faintly visible: rotating habitats, forests, lakes, and transparent corridors curving along the inner rim where people walk. At the center axis, a transparent gravity-balance pod floats like an eye watching over its own world. At the stern, fusion engines emit a thin pale-blue plasma exhaust that stretches silently for hundreds of kilometers in vacuum. In the background, a violet-blue nebula, bright stars, and a tilted Milky Way. The cold-toned cosmos contrasts with the warm living light inside the ship. Far away, a small blue planet — the home they can never return to.

Epic composition, hard sci-fi engineering aesthetic, cinematic, HDR, exquisite detail.

### Negative
blurry, low quality, cartoon, flat lighting, empty interior, overexposed stars, toy-like

## ③ 人机共生日常 —「AI 不是奴仆,是家人」

### 中文版
近未来的清晨,薄雾尚未散去,阳光透过落地窗洒进一间朴素而温暖的居所。木质家具、手工陶杯、墙上一幅拙拙的水彩画——生活的痕迹。一位约四十岁的男人坐在窗边藤椅上,双手捧着热咖啡,目光平静而放松。他身旁约一臂之远处,悬浮着一团柔和的淡蓝色光雾,直径约半米,那是他的 AI 伴侣;光雾内部隐约浮现半透明的几何图案——三角、圆、流线——像思绪泛起时的涟漪,缓慢优雅地变幻。光雾底部有一道极细的光丝垂落到地,仿佛在"站立"。窗外是绿色植被覆盖的城市天际线,空中漂浮着其他家庭的同款光点,像一盏盏温柔的灯笼。整个画面没有一根电线、一块屏幕、一个机械关节——科技隐没在生活里。

温暖、宁静、亲密的家庭氛围,柔焦,晨光,水彩般的通透感,电影感,情感细腻,构图以人与光雾的关系为核心。

### English Prompt
Early morning in the near future, thin mist still lingering, sunlight streaming through floor-to-ceiling windows into a plain yet warm home. Wooden furniture, a handmade ceramic mug, a simple watercolor painting on the wall — traces of a real life. A man in his forties sits in a rattan chair by the window, holding hot coffee in both hands, his expression calm and at ease. Beside him, about an arm's length away, floats a soft pale-blue mist of light, half a meter across — his AI companion. Within the mist, faint translucent geometric patterns — triangles, circles, flowing lines — shift slowly and elegantly, like ripples of thought. A single thin filament of light descends from the mist to the floor, as if it were standing. Outside the window, a green-covered city skyline; in the sky, similar gentle points of light float above other homes, like warm lanterns. No wires, no screens, no mechanical joints anywhere — technology has dissolved into life.

Warm, serene, intimate family atmosphere, soft focus, morning light, watercolor-like clarity, cinematic, emotionally nuanced, composition centered on the relationship between human and light.

### Negative
cold, clinical, robots, screens, wires, dystopian, sterile hospital lighting, evil AI, threatening

## ④ 恒星边缘的文明 —「把恒星装进口袋」

### 中文版
一颗暗红色的红矮星悬在画面中央偏左,光芒微弱而古老,像是垂暮的巨人。环绕它的是一圈极其细密的淡金色薄纱——那是由数以百万计的太阳能卫星、居住环和光帆组成的戴森云,绵延数亿公里,像一层会呼吸的皮肤包裹着恒星。戴森云边缘处,一座巨大的环形居住站在缓缓自转,表面城市灯火如微尘般闪烁,像夜色中的海岸线。镜头从这座居住站逐渐拉远:先看到它孤独地悬浮,然后是周围的卫星群,最后是整片戴森云的壮阔全景——恒星的光芒被部分遮蔽,在云层间隙中透出一道道细长的光柱,像教堂彩窗。画面最远处,银河横亘,而近处一座小的人造卫星城市上,依稀可见灯光组成的街道网格与一座亮着灯的穹顶公园。

极远景构图,壮阔,孤寂又温柔,硬科幻与宇宙浪漫主义结合,高动态范围,细节如微缩模型。

### English Prompt
A dim red dwarf star hangs slightly left of center, its light ancient and faint, like a dying giant. Around it, a remarkably fine veil of pale gold — a Dyson cloud composed of millions of solar satellites, habitat rings, and light sails, spanning hundreds of millions of kilometers, like a breathing skin wrapped around the star. At the cloud's edge, a huge ring habitat rotates slowly, its city lights twinkling like dust, like a coastline seen at night. The camera slowly pulls back from this habitat: first it floats alone, then surrounded by satellite clusters, finally revealing the full panorama of the Dyson cloud — the star's light partially blocked, thin shafts of light piercing through gaps in the cloud like stained-glass cathedral windows. In the far distance, the Milky Way stretches across; in the foreground, a small artificial satellite city shows street grids of light and a glowing domed park.

Extreme wide shot, majestic, lonely yet tender, hard sci-fi blended with cosmic romanticism, HDR, miniature-model-like detail.

### Negative
dark void only, boring black space, no stars, oversimplified, cartoon, blurry, empty
