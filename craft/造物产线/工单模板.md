# 造物产线工单模板

一次工单只处理一个角色和一个可验收目标。下游 agent 不通读世界档案，不复制长日志，不修改未授权文件。

```yaml
job_id: WO-YYYYMMDD-###
role: fact | derive | interface | geometry | validate | review | present
context: C0 + C1:<asset-family> + C2:<asset>
goal: one measurable outcome
inputs: [paths or fact_ids]
mutable: [fields this job may change]
frozen: [fields this job must not change]
outputs: [paths]
checks: [commands]
escalate_if: [canon_conflict, shared_interface_change, repeated_failure]
return: "STATUS/ARTIFACTS/MEASURED/CHECKS/LIMITS/NEXT; <=15 lines"
```

输出状态只用 `PASS`、`FAIL`、`BLOCKED_INPUT`。没有来源、推导式或假设编号的数值不能进入正式参数；工程估算不能写成专业认证。
